# Aquasample

## Idea
A game about learning aquatic ecology through finding and inspecting various creatures from a freshwater stream sample using a sampling tray.

1. Players scoop a net sample from a virtual stream tank and tip it into a tray and a variety of creatures spill out and wriggle around.
2. The player then learn ways to identify the creature they are inspecting (holding in their hand) by being quizzed on the organism's features.
3. If they get a feature wrong, they are shown the correct answer and facts about the organism are displayed once the creature is identified.
4. Once the creature is identified, they are placed in a testing jar where players are quizzed along with a fact card to determine stream health.
5. Wrong answers explain why.
6. A stream health score is displayed.
